Charles Iliya Krempeaux's Marriage

by Charles Iliya Krempeaux

Charles Iliya Krempeaux is married to Hyerim Kang (강혜림). Hyerim goes by the name Bella, as Bella is the English translation of the Korean name Hyerim (혜림).

Charles and Bella married in July 2019 in downtown Vancouver.
