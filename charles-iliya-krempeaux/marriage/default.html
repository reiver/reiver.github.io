Charles Iliya Krempeaux's Marriage

by Charles Iliya Krempeaux

Charles Iliya Krempeaux is married to Hyerim Kang (강혜림).

Hyerim usually goes by the name Bella, as Bella is an English translation of the Korean name Hyerim (혜림).

Charles and Bella first met on Saturday November 11th, 2018, got engaged in June 2019, and married in July 2019 in downtown Vancouver.

Children

Charles and Bella have 2 children — an older son, a younger daughter.

Wedding Ceremony

Charles and Bella had 2 wedding ceremonies — the first one in Vancouver (Canada), and the second one in Korea.

Their first wedding ceremony in Vancouver (Canada) was a small wedding ceremony where they became legally married — and was attended by Charles' mother and brother. (Charles' father died more-than a decade because Charles and Bella got married.)

Their second wedding ceremony in Korea was a large traditional Korean wedding ceremony attended Bella's family, friends, and others. Because of travel restrictions during the COVID-19 lock downs, the second wedding ceremony happened quite a bit after the first wedding ceremony.
